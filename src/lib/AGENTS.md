Website-managed Telegram workers use the service-only telegram_worker_bots registry with AES-GCM credentials under TELEGRAM_CREDENTIAL_KEY; admin-checked functions expose only non-secret fields. Dynamic worker webhooks authenticate before reads, Gaffer dispatches group tasks centrally to prevent bot loops, atomic set_telegram_worker assigns per-chat workers, and trade_signals.sender_worker keeps edits and outcome replies on the sending bot.

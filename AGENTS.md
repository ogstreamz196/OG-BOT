- Signal learning (src/lib/signal-learning.server.ts, site_content "signals.learning"): OG B0T learns from its own setups. When the minute watcher had nothing to send (events 0), at most every 2 hours (site_content "signals.learning.lastRun" claimed first), it studies one market (gold via the XAUUSDT perpetual, PAXGUSDT if that's down, or bitcoin, whichever was studied longest ago) from a growing history (src/lib/signal-samples.server.ts, table signal_learn_samples: every replayed setup is kept, tuning-free, keyed market/version/t; each study replays only what's new since the last plus BACKFILL_DAYS (7) further back until LEARN_DAYS (90) are covered, learns from everything kept, and deletes samples over 120 days; bump SAMPLE_VERSION when buildPlan or collectSamples change what a sample holds; without the table it replays the last 14 days as before): collectSamples (trade-signal-backtest.ts) replays 5-minute setups with the live engine, keeping each plan's raw levels (TradePlan.raw), then evaluate() tries other Tuning settings on exactly those setups with the tracker's rules (result in the engine's own risk units). Tuning (trade-signal.ts) is what it controls: stopRoom (default 1.2, bounds 1.0–1.6), the three target pull-ins (default 0.8/0.6/0.85, bounds in BOUNDS) and minConfidence (default 51, bounds 51–80; below it a setup is a wait) and pullback (how far a pullback limit entry waits back towards the entry-frame 20 EMA: 0 the price now, 1 the EMA itself; default 0.5, because full-EMA pullbacks left about half of limit orders unfilled; the classic rules keep 1; TradePlan.raw.pull lets the learner move a past setup's entry, risk and targets together). Two filters are learned too: minVol (bounds 0–0.8, default 0 = off; a setup is a "Too quiet" wait when volatilityRatio, the entry chart's 14-candle ATR against its 100-candle one, is below it; nudged like the other numbers) and skipBlocks (UK 4-hour blocks from ukBlock, 0 = 00:00–04:00 … 5 = 20:00–24:00; after each study losingBlocks sits out up to two blocks with at least 10 trades that won under 40% and lost on average, worst first, only when sitting them out beats trading them; a skipped block is a "losing lately" wait). TradePlan.raw carries vol and block so past setups can be filtered the same way. A new setting is kept only if it beats the current one on the first 70% of the period by a margin, isn't worse on the last 30% it never saw, and has at least 20 filled trades; it then moves halfway there (the full step only when halfway isn't enough), always inside BOUNDS. Live results then overrule the replay for the confidence floor (liveFloor): the market's last 30 days of trade_signals (not bias calls) are split into confidence bands (51–59, 60–69, 70–74, 75–79); working up from the bottom, a band with at least 6 settled trades (won = any target, lost = stopped) that won under 40% lifts minConfidence above it, and the first band doing fine stops the climb; it only ever raises the floor, inside BOUNDS. Each study writes a plain lesson and keeps the last 12 runs. Every filled trade in a study pays a typical spread (SPREAD_COST: gold 0.35, bitcoin 15, set on each sample's cost and taken off its R), so small take profits are judged on what they bank. Each study also learns a setting per UK session (learnSessions; SESSIONS in trade-signal.ts: Asian 00–07, London 07–12, New York 12–17, evening 17–24, ukSession) from that session's setups alone, with the same keep-only-if-it-holds-up rules, only for sessions with at least MIN_TRADES trades, never below the live floor; MarketLearning.sessions. Live signals and market-bias calls use the learned setting for their market and session (learnedTuning(market, at): the session's own, else the market's, cached 5 minutes, hand-set rules when nothing's learned); the OG Desk reads the lesson with the chart; signalResultsNote gives the lessons to OG B0T when people ask how the signals are doing. Prices are still code-computed, never AI-written.

- Battle Zone learns slang via service-only `learn_battle_words`; OG Bot openers come from the service-only catchphrase bank.
- Trade signals (messenger + Telegram): src/lib/trade-signal-context.server.ts detects a market + trade request, pulls live candles (Binance/Kraken for crypto and gold via PAXG, Yahoo for forex, indices, oil, $stocks) and computes indicators and the plan in code (src/lib/trade-signal.ts); every signal names the most likely direction with a confidence % (indicator agreement, never a win rate) and a UK-time "valid until" (scalp 1h, intraday 4h, swing 24h; Telegram signal replies self-destruct one 5-minute clean-up sweep before that); the model only presents those numbers and must say so when no data loads, never invent levels. Each signal also checks the live ticker just before sending (market entries use it), rates setup quality (Clean / Mixed / Choppy: ADX under 20 on the entry and middle frames means no clean setup), warns about high-impact news from the free weekly calendar for the market's currencies, and for crypto adds volume, Binance futures funding and open interest (src/lib/trade-signal-extras.ts); these extras are soft and capped at about 1.5s so they never delay a signal. Telegram signals are recorded in trade_signals and followed by a once-a-minute watcher (/api/public/trade-signal-watch → src/lib/trade-signal-tracker.server.ts, rules in src/lib/trade-signal-outcome.ts: closed 1-minute candles only, stop counts first when a candle touches both, stop moves to entry after TP1): members get a reply when the entry fills, a target or the stop is hit, the higher-timeframe trend flips before TP1, or the entry never fills (running out of time posts nothing, only the status changes); only one "entry not reached" signal ever shows in a chat: its notice id is kept in feeds.notice, and the next one deletes the older one's card and notice (keepOneUnfilled) and clears its chat_id, and the card carries a live price line. New signals show a 30-day track record for that market and timeframe once 5 have filled; the confidence % stays indicator agreement, never a win rate. Headlines from the Yahoo Finance search feed, soft-fail. Before the analysis the user gets an instant persona-toned "hold on" line from the shared catchphrase bank (kind trade_hold, pick_trade_hold via src/lib/trade-hold.server.ts): each user only ever gets lines they haven't seen, low supply adds AI-written lines and an exhausted user gets fresh fragment-built lines, both added for everyone; built-in holdingMessage is only the outage fallback (app fetches it via getTradeHoldLine, Telegram sends it first); it carries an invisible tag and is never fed back as chat history. In the app, signal replies carry a hidden [[SIGNAL_EXPIRES:…]] tag (from chatOgBot's signal_expires_at); once it passes the signal and its holding line collapse into an "expired, get a fresh signal" card, and cleanTradeHistory replaces expired signals in AI history so stale levels are never reused.
